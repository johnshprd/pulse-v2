Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pulse ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Pulse website and app at mpspulse.app (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your email address and an encrypted password. Authentication is handled by Supabase, our backend provider.

Nutrition data. We store the food entries, protein amounts, meal times, daily goals, and custom foods that you log in the app, so that your data is available across your devices.

Payment information. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not see or store your card details. Stripe shares with us your email address and subscription status so we can activate your account. Stripe's privacy policy is available at stripe.com/privacy.

Meal photos. If you use the meal photo scan feature, your photo is sent securely to an AI service (Anthropic) to estimate protein content. Photos are processed to provide the result and are not used to train AI models. We do not permanently store your meal photos.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the protein tracking service and sync your data across devices
  • To manage your subscription and free trial
  • To respond to support requests
  • To improve the app's features and reliability

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and nutrition data are stored securely with Supabase in the United Kingdom/European region (eu-west-2). Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to operate Pulse:

  • Supabase — account authentication and data storage
  • Stripe — subscription payments
  • Anthropic — AI meal photo analysis
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting
  • Cloudflare — DNS and security

5. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us at johnshpd@gmail.com. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location (including under UK GDPR), you have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise any of these rights.

7. Children

Pulse is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be noted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at johnshpd@gmail.com.
